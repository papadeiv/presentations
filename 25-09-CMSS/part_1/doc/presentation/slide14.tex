        \frametitle{Forcing tipping as a policymaker}

        % Body
        \footnotesize

        We further simplify our model by imposing $\alpha = 1 - \beta$ but  at the same time we make it non-autonomous by letting $\beta = \lambda(t) = \frac{1}{2}(\text{tanh}(\varepsilon t + \delta) + 1)$
        \begin{equation*}
            \dot{x}=x(1-x)(x-\lambda(t))\,. 
        \end{equation*}

        \vspace{1mm}

        If we act fast enough (i.e. we pick $\varepsilon$ large) we can make some trajectories to tip to the favorable strategy.
